# Safety templates

Editable safety templates in German, French and English. Use the files for one language together. The Word files include the procedure, quick reference and working forms; the roadmap is provided in both Excel and PowerPoint.

## Using the register

Replace the example records before entering company events. Give each event a unique reference and keep it unchanged. Enter records from row 4; calculated fields, dropdowns and summaries are prepared through row 1000.

Set the reporting year and annual hours worked in the KPI sheet. Hours must cover the same workforce and year as the incidents included. In these workbooks, LTIF counts lost-time and fatal injuries per million hours. The severity rate counts days lost per million hours. Near misses remain in the internal register.

Use the choice lists for category fields. Keep category names consistent with existing records, and retain the specific incident facts in the description and cause-detail fields.

## Using the forms and roadmap

Work permits cover the general job assessment, hot work, work at height, confined spaces and energy isolation. Complete the relevant checklists and named assignments before work starts. The confined-space and isolation forms include identified continuation pages for logs and sign-offs.

The incident form records the facts, timeline, causes, actions and follow-up. Assign each action to a person and check its effectiveness.

The roadmap contains example topics and actions. Adapt them to the company, define the target and baseline, and schedule the actions. Use the same KPI definitions in the workbook and slides.
